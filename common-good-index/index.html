Each card puts two people or groups side by side, living or dead, from any period: nations, empires, companies, organizations, movements, dynasties. Pick the one you think has done more for the common good: swipe the card left or right, or use the buttons. Swipe up to skip. On a keyboard, the left and right arrows pick, = or space is a tie, and the down arrow skips. Every pick moves both ratings by standard Elo (K = 32), so beating a highly rated contender counts for more than beating an unknown one; a tie counts like a draw in chess.

The contenders come from Wikidata, chosen by how many Wikipedia language editions and sister sites cover them. That makes them recognisable, and it also inherits Wikipedia's biases toward the English-speaking, Western, recent and male. Coverage decides who is eligible, never anyone's rating: everyone starts at 1500. Names, descriptions and pictures come from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, free images only; each picture links to its file page for the author and licence.
