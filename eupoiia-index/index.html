Bradley Charles Cooper (born January 5, 1975) is an American actor and filmmaker. He is the recipient of various accolades, including a British Academy Film Award and three Grammy Awards. In addition, he has been nominated for twelve Academy Awards, six Golden Globe Awards, and a Tony Award. Cooper appeared on the Forbes Celebrity 100 list three times and on Time's list of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2015. His films have grossed $13 billion worldwide, and he has been placed in annual rankings of the world's highest-paid actors four times.

Salvador Allende

28th president of Chile (1908–1973)

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

Salvador Guillermo Allende Gossens (26 June 1908 – 11 September 1973) was a Chilean politician who served as the 29th president of Chile from 1970 until his suicide during the 1973 Chilean coup d'état. He has been described as the first Marxist to be elected president in a liberal democracy in Latin America. Due to his commitment to democracy in the pursuit of socialism, and the manner of his death and deposition, he is revered as a hero and martyr by many socialists worldwide, across many different strains of beliefs, to a degree unusual in socialist politics.

Vanuatu, officially the Republic of Vanuatu (French: République de Vanuatu; Bislama: Ripablik blong Vanuatu), is an archipelagic country in the Melanesia region of Oceania located in the South Pacific Ocean. The archipelago, which is of volcanic origin, is 1,750 km (1,090 mi) east of northern Australia, 540 km (340 mi) northeast of New Caledonia, east of New Guinea, southeast of Solomon Islands, and west of Fiji.

Each card represents two people or groups, living or dead, from any period: nations, empires, companies, organizations, movements, dynasties. Pick the one you think has done more for the common good: swipe the card left or right, or use the buttons. Swipe up for Skip. On a keyboard, the left and right arrows pick, = or space is a tie, and the down arrow chooses Skip. Every pick moves both ratings by standard Elo (K = 32), so beating a highly rated contender counts for more than beating an unknown one; a tie counts like a draw in chess.

The contenders come from Wikidata, chosen by how many Wikipedia language editions and sister sites cover them. That makes them recognizable, and it also inherits Wikipedia's biases toward the English-speaking, Western, recent and male. Coverage decides who is eligible, never anyone's rating: everyone starts at 1500. Names, descriptions and pictures come from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, free images only; each picture links to its file page for the author and license.
