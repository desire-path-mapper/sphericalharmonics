Shirvan

historical region in Azerbaijan

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

Shirvan (from Classical Persian شروان Shirwān or شیروان Shīrwān [Iranian Persian: شروان Shervân or شیروان Shirvân]; Azerbaijani: Şirvan; Tat: Şirvan) is a historical Iranian region in the eastern Caucasus, as known both during the pre-Islamic Sasanian Era and the early Islamic period. Today, the region is an industrially and agriculturally developed part of the Republic of Azerbaijan that stretches between the western shores of the Caspian Sea and the Kura River, centered on the Shirvan Plain.

Cook Islands

country in the South Pacific Ocean in free association with New Zealand

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

The Cook Islands is an island country in Polynesia, part of Oceania, in the South Pacific Ocean. While self-governing, the country is in free association with New Zealand. It consists of 15 islands whose total land area is approximately 236.7 square kilometres (91 sq mi). The Cook Islands Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) covers 1,960,027 square kilometres (756,771 sq mi) of ocean. The capital is Avarua, located on the island of Rarotonga.

Federico Fellini (Italian: [fedeˈriːko felˈliːni]; 20 January 1920 – 31 October 1993) was an Italian film director and screenwriter known for his distinctive style that blends fantasy and baroque images with earthiness. Recognized as one of the greatest and most influential filmmakers of all time, his films rank highly in critical polls such as that of Cahiers du Cinéma and Sight & Sound, which lists his 1963 film 8½ as the 10th-greatest film of all time.

Each card represents two people or groups, living or dead, from any period: nations, empires, companies, organizations, movements, dynasties. Pick the one you think has done more for the common good: swipe the card left or right, or use the buttons. Swipe up for Skip. On a keyboard, the left and right arrows pick, = or space is a tie, and the down arrow chooses Skip. Every pick moves both ratings by standard Elo (K = 32), so beating a highly rated contender counts for more than beating an unknown one; a tie counts like a draw in chess.

The contenders come from Wikidata, chosen by how many Wikipedia language editions and sister sites cover them. That makes them recognizable, and it also inherits Wikipedia's biases toward the English-speaking, Western, recent and male. Coverage decides who is eligible, never anyone's rating: everyone starts at 1500. Names, descriptions and pictures come from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, free images only; each picture links to its file page for the author and license.
